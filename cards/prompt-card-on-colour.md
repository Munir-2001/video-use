- **what:** The whole frame flips to one flat brand colour with a faint halftone-dot texture. A title with an accent word sits top-centre; below it a white rounded card where a prompt or instruction types in character by character with a blinking cursor; a muted "+5 >" to the right hints there are more. The speaker is a round PiP in the bottom-right corner over the colour. It is the "here is exactly what I typed" beat and it reads as a deliberate break from the dark look.
- **use when:** I quote a prompt, a rule or a checklist line I actually typed; a "so try this" moment that needs to feel different from the rest of the reel.
- **avoid when:** More than ~12 words (it will not fit a vertical card at readable size); back-to-back use (it loses its punch); when the text is confidential client data.
