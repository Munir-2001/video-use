# Comment bubbles beside the talking head

- **kind:** style
- **from:** ref-05 of the 2026-11-16 drop (cards/_inbox/ref-05.png)
- **what:** Over the full-frame talking head, two or three white rounded comment cards float either side of the face, each with a small grey avatar circle top-left, a dark pill with a heart and a count top-right, and a short quoted sentence in bold dark text (2–3 lines). They stack staggered so they never cover the eyes or mouth. Reads as "what people said" social proof while the speaker reacts.

- **avoid when:** More than 3 bubbles; the quotes are longer than ~14 words; the face is framed tight so there is no clear space beside it.
