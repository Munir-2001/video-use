- **kind:** motion
- **from:** ref-01, 03, 04, 06, 08, 09..11, 13, 15..22 of the 2026-11-16 drop (cards/_inbox/ref-NN.png, frames of one reference YouTube video); 2026-11-16 merged: ref-ig-carousel-01..09.png (editorial title/end card variant, stills of one Instagram carousel)
- **what:** Every section of the reference opens with a 2–4 word headline: heavy sans, one word set apart in a second typeface and the brand's accent colour (the "why" word: "code", "first", "system", "prompt"). Words land one at a time on the beat of speech (ref-09 shows the first two words, ref-10 the rest 5 s later), then the title sits still while the graphic below it builds. Optional small tracked-uppercase kicker above (a one-word label), and a chapter variant: a huge accent numeral plus "step N of M" kicker to the right (ref-03). Title sits top-third when a graphic follows, or bottom-centre when the graphic is the hero (ref-17, 18). Editorial card variant (carousel): a dark warm background, a two-line headline where line 1 is heavy sans in white and line 2 is a contrasting italic serif in cream ("One thing. *A second idea.*"), a mono uppercase kicker row with a series label at left and "06 / 09" at right, a huge roman numeral on a small card, a starburst mark, a black terminal-style mini card with a checklist (tick, item, dim description), and a cover/end slide with one giant numeral. Static design language from stills; reference is cream-on-brown with an orange star.
- **use when:** The start of a new idea in a talking-head reel: I name the section, then a graphic explains it. Also for a one-line punchline when I lean on a single word.
- **use when (editorial):** the first frame (cover) or last frame (end card) of a series reel, "part 3 of 6", or a quiet payoff where I am not on screen.
- **avoid when:** I am mid-sentence with no beat to land on; when captions already carry the same words (double text); more than one title per 3 s.
- **prompt:** Vertical 1080x1920, background #0B0B0F (or over my footage with a 40 % #0B0B0F scrim behind the text only). Headline in Inter ExtraBold (brand/fonts/Inter-ExtraBold.ttf), 112 px, sentence case, #F5F5F7, max 80 % width, 2 lines max, anchored at y = 0.18 (top third) or y = 0.56 when a hero graphic sits above; never inside the caption band (y 0.64–0.72), never over my face, never in the bottom 20 %. Exactly one word in #F4502A (if the sentence has a second emphasis, use #1F5BFF for it, never two accents). Optional kicker 2 lines above: Inter SemiBold 36 px, letter-spacing 0.18 em, #A0A3AD, uppercase allowed only for this kicker. Motion: each word pops in on its spoken onset, scale 0.92→1.0 and opacity 0→1 in 350 ms cubic, no overshoot, no blur, no crossfade; hold; exit all words together 250 ms.
