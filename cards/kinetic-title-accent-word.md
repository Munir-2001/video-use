- **prompt:** Vertical 1080x1920, background #0B0B0F (or over my footage with a 40 % #0B0B0F scrim behind the text only). Motion: each word pops in on its spoken onset, scale 0.92→1.0 and opacity 0→1 in 350 ms cubic, no overshoot, no blur, no crossfade; hold; exit all words together 250 ms.
