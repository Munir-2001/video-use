- **what:** Split-top layout: the top half of the frame is a panel with a faint dot grid that holds every graphic, the speaker fills the bottom half. Hook (0–2 s): a title + grey subtitle sit above ~50 small rounded icon tiles arranged to draw the digits of the number in the title; the digits *build* tile by tile (about half the tiles at 0.0 s, all by 1.0 s, seen). On the spoken word that widens the claim, every tile flies to its slot in a 5x10 grid and grows slightly: 250 ms (6 frames at 24 fps), ease-out, no bounce. Chapters (from ~3.9 s): a mono kicker "[ 01 / 03 ]" + bold title + accent count pill appear above the grid; the whole grid dims to ~25 % except the chapter's tiles, which get a 2 px accent rounded outline (the highlight swap is instant, ≤2 frames). Inside a chapter, each named item pops a white item card over the lower grid (mono URL chip above, logo tile, bold name in the item's colour, grey descriptor, accent rating pill top-right) on its spoken name, 170 ms scale 0.8→1 + fade. The next named item swaps in: the old card slides up ~60 px and fades while the new one rises into the same spot, 250 ms total; a second card style shows a mini page preview with a mono "[ source · site ]" label. On a chapter change the card hard-cuts away, the kicker swaps hard, the title fades in over 125 ms and the count pill pops ~170 ms later. The reel ends with a hard cut back to the opening number formation, so the loop is seamless. Something changes every 0.6–1.5 s.
