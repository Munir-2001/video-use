# Window mockup that slides up and fills

- **kind:** motion
- **from:** ref-11..13 and ref-18 of the 2026-11-16 drop (cards/_inbox/ref-NN.png)
- **what:** A rounded dark "app window" (three traffic-light dots, 12 px radius, soft shadow) slides up into the frame under the title. It arrives mostly empty; its contents then fill in sections over the next 3–6 s: a thumbnail grid, a column of coloured "code" bars that type in line by line, slider knobs that travel to their value, a mini timeline whose clips extend left to right with a playhead sweeping (ref-12 → ref-11). A lighter variant (ref-18) is a document card: filename header, a tag pill top-right, grey placeholder lines, and ONE row highlighted with a tinted band and a monospace line. Supporting chips (a "free" pill, a logo) pop in beside the window (ref-13).
- **use when:** I describe a tool, a file, a workflow or "what the system did" and have no real screen recording I am allowed to show (private screens are banned). Also for showing a single important line from a file.
- **avoid when:** I have a real, safe screen recording; the sequence would need more than one new element per 3 s; the window would cover the caption band.
- **prompt:** Vertical 1080x1920, background #0B0B0F. Window: 920x700 px, #15161C fill, 24 px radius, 1 px stroke #2A2B33, three 10 px dots top-left in #A0A3AD, shadow 0 24 64 rgba(0,0,0,.6). Place it at y 0.22–0.40 (below a title, above the caption band) or y 0.60–0.78 (below the caption band), keep 140 px clear at the right edge. Enter: slide from y+160 px to rest with opacity 0→1, 350 ms cubic, no bounce. Fill order, each step ≥ 3 s apart and tied to what I am saying: (1) content bars (Inter-free, pure rectangles 10 px tall, 6 px radius) draw left→right in 250 ms, colours cycling #4CC9F0, #FFD166, #A0A3AD; (2) sliders: track #2A2B33, knob #FFD166 travels to value in 300 ms; (3) mini timeline: 4 rows, clips extend left→right 400 ms, playhead #EF476F sweeps once. Document variant: header text in JetBrains Mono Medium 30 px #A0A3AD, tag pill #FFD166 on #0B0B0F text, highlighted row band rgba(255,209,102,.14) with a 4 px #FFD166 left bar and the line in JetBrains Mono 36 px #F5F5F7. Chips: 44 px pill, #FFD166 fill, Inter SemiBold 32 px #0B0B0F, pop scale 0.8→1 in 250 ms. One soft "tick" SFX only on the highlighted row. Exit: all 250 ms down and out. Captions go on top.
