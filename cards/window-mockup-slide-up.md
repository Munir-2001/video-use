# Window mockup that slides up and fills

- **kind:** motion
- **from:** ref-11..13 and ref-18 of the 2026-11-16 drop (cards/_inbox/ref-NN.png)
- **what:** A rounded dark "app window" (three traffic-light dots, 12 px radius, soft shadow) slides up into the frame under the title. It arrives mostly empty; its contents then fill in sections over the next 3–6 s: a thumbnail grid, a column of coloured "code" bars that type in line by line, slider knobs that travel to their value, a mini timeline whose clips extend left to right with a playhead sweeping (ref-12 → ref-11). A lighter variant (ref-18) is a document card: filename header, a tag pill top-right, grey placeholder lines, and ONE row highlighted with a tinted band and a monospace line. Supporting chips (a "free" pill, a logo) pop in beside the window (ref-13).
- **use when:** I describe a tool, a file, a workflow or "what the system did". With a real, safe screen recording use the **live screen variant**: the recording plays inside the window above my head and, on the word that names something ("scraping", a repo name, a skill), the view inside the window pushes in on that element. Without a recording, use the drawn fill-in version. Also for showing a single important line from a file.
- **avoid when:** The recording shows private data (banned); the sequence would need more than one new element per 3 s; the window would cover the caption band.
- **prompt:** Vertical 1080x1920, background #0B0B0F. Window: 920x700 px, #15161C fill, 24 px radius, 1 px stroke #2A2B33, three 10 px dots top-left in #A0A3AD, shadow 0 24 64 rgba(0,0,0,.6). Place it at y 0.22–0.40 (below a title, above the caption band) or y 0.60–0.78 (below the caption band), keep 140 px clear at the right edge. Enter: slide from y+160 px to rest with opacity 0→1, 350 ms cubic, no bounce. Fill order, each step ≥ 3 s apart and tied to what I am saying: (1) content bars (Inter-free, pure rectangles 10 px tall, 6 px radius) draw left→right in 250 ms, colours cycling #1F5BFF, #F4502A, #A0A3AD; (2) sliders: track #2A2B33, knob #F4502A travels to value in 300 ms; (3) mini timeline: 4 rows, clips extend left→right 400 ms, playhead #EF476F sweeps once. Document variant: header text in JetBrains Mono Medium 30 px #A0A3AD, tag pill #F4502A on #0B0B0F text, highlighted row band rgba(244,80,42,.14) with a 4 px #F4502A left bar and the line in JetBrains Mono 36 px #F5F5F7. Chips: 44 px pill, #F4502A fill, Inter SemiBold 32 px #0B0B0F, pop scale 0.8→1 in 250 ms. One soft "tick" SFX only on the highlighted row. Exit: all 250 ms down and out. Captions go on top.

- **live screen variant (zoom beats):** Layout split_top or face_corner. The window (same frame as above) holds a `<video>` of the screen clip from `footage.in_s`, muted, `object-fit: cover`, with the real cursor visible. Enter: window slides up 350 ms, the clip already playing inside. **Zoom beats** from the planner (`zoom_beats: [{word, t, box: [x, y, w, h]}]`, box as fractions of the screen): on each beat the inner view scales 1 → 2.2 (or whatever makes the box fill 70 % of the window) and translates so the box is centred, 350 ms cubic ease-out, no overshoot; a 3 px #F4502A rounded ring draws around the box 150 ms after landing; hold ≥ 1.5 s; the next beat moves directly to the next box (250 ms); the last beat eases back to 1.0 before exit, or the shot hard-cuts. Zooms live inside the window only; the window frame and my face never move. One soft whoosh per push-in, max 4 per window. Exit: 250 ms down and out, or a hard cut on the stretch end.

- **screenshot variant (assets/):** the window holds a real screenshot from `videos/<folder>/assets/` (the repo page, the config, a session), top-aligned, and the camera moves: push in 350 ms on the named element with a 3 px flame outline, hold ≥ 1.5 s, pan 250 ms to the next, ease back before the window leaves. While holding, the window drifts (−8 px, scale to 1.02) and a drawn cursor moves between targets. Preferred over the drawn fill-in version whenever an asset exists.
